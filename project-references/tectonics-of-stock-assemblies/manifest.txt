MEDIA SELECTION
---------------
For the portfolio project page, first identify:

1. strongest final hero image
2. 1–2 images explaining the stock-analysis stage
3. strongest clustering/data visualisation
4. structural-field / structural-analysis visual
5. aggregation/process animation
6. fabrication or assembly animation/video if relevant
7. final prototype/result imagery
8. only essential Grasshopper/process screenshots

Do NOT overload the page.

The website should communicate the project clearly rather than reproduce the full presentation.

GIF / VIDEO
-----------
GIFs and MP4 files may be used directly where motion communicates something that
a still image cannot.

Examples:
- aggregation growth

- optimisation/evolution
- robotic/fabrication process

Do not replace useful motion media with screenshots unless technically necessary.

Before copying a video/GIF to the website, inspect it and identify what it shows.
