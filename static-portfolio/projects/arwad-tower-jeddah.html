Arwad Tower, Jeddah

Office
Studio Symbiosis
Year
2023–2024
Location
Jeddah

Arwad Tower is a commercial office development near Jeddah Airport combining flexible office floors, commercial spaces, parking, a full-height atrium, open terraces and informal meeting areas.

Summer working-hour temperatures exceed approximately 45°C. This environmental constraint connected the massing study to daylight, radiation, shading and facade geometry.

A parametric option space

A catalogue was developed by varying core placement, commercial-space distribution, parking layouts, tower form and atrium configuration. Alternatives were evaluated through built-up area and daylight performance.

Seasonal radiation study

Radiation was studied across cardinal corners and seasonal dates to inform orientation, overhang and vertical shading responses.
