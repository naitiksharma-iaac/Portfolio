This structural catalogue tests a family of pedestrian shell bridges for a 48-metre span in Dharavi.

Three geometric studies compare symmetric, asymmetric and three-legged systems through common load cases and performance metrics, making trade-offs visible rather than selecting form by appearance alone.

A structural design space

The catalogue compares three-legged and four-legged shells, symmetric and asymmetric support configurations, and different surface-area distributions as samples within a larger bridge design space.

Relaxed and inflated meshes

Flat meshes are relaxed and inflated with Kangaroo to create smooth doubly curved shells, then transferred into Karamba for structural analysis.

No local project media is currently present in the repository. The preserved source folder remains ready for original media.
