BioPol 3D Printing

Institution
CITA — Royal Danish Academy
Year
2026

BioPol 3D Printing investigates robotic extrusion with bio-based materials through a fabrication-informed computational design workflow. Geometry, material behaviour, toolpath generation, robotic fabrication and machine learning are treated as an interdependent system.

The study works through print velocity, material flow, density, pressure, collapse thresholds, overhang, infill geometry and non-planar slicing.

Parameters that shape printability

Initial studies isolate parameters that influence deposition and stability, forming a shared language between geometric design, material tests and robotic toolpath planning.

Task, material and fabrication constraints

The workflow establishes a parametric relationship between task, material and fabrication constraints. Material behaviour affects structural stability.
