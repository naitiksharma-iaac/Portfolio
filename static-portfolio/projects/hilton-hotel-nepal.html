Role
Computational design, facade rationalisation, shop drawings and fabrication development

Hilton Hotel, Nepal uses expressive GFRC scissor bands integrated with a curtain-wall facade. My contribution focused on rationalising the curved band geometry and translating it into coordinated, transportable and installable components.

The computational workflow connected the design geometry to shop drawings, CNC-cut mould frameworks, assembly information and construction. Fabrication constraints informed how the facade was resolved.

Within the wider Studio Symbiosis team, this was a technical delivery role within a collaborative project, not sole design authorship.

Site and cleat coordination

The facade bands were checked and remodelled where required against site measurements, cleat positions and the mild-steel support frame.

Perpendicular sections as fabrication geometry

Multiple perpendicular section planes were distributed along the curved surface to segment the scissor bands into components that could be transported and installed.

No local project media is currently present in the repository. The preserved source folder remains ready for original media.
