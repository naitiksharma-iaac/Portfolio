Term

Location
Dharavi, Mumbai, India

Above & Inbetween proposes an eco-parametric infrastructure that grows above and between Dharavi’s existing informal buildings without replacing the neighbourhood fabric.

Two-dimensional and spatial Cellular Automata generate elevated floor plates and a bamboo lattice in response to access, rooftop use, solar infrastructure, agriculture and local structural conditions.

Working with existing urban fabric

The study records corrugated roofs, tarpaulin, mild-steel frames, bamboo scaffolding, timber, water drums, vertical circulation, roof occupation, utilities, recycling networks, workshops and shared spaces. The site is treated as an active material and social system, not empty land.

Local rules and emergent form

Dharavi’s morphology can be understood as emerging from many local decisions rather than one central masterplan. A Cellular Automata model similarly produces larger patterns through repeated local rules, with cell state determined by neighbouring cells.

No local project media is currently present in the repository. The preserved source folder remains ready for original media.
