01 / Robotic drawing study

Following a line

Simulation and physical drawing

A drawing provides a direct way to understand the relationship between a programmed curve and the movement of the robot. The simulation shows the complete motion; the close-up isolates the toolpath. The physical trial records the robot tracing that path on a drawing surface.

02 / Tectonics of Stock Assemblies

Machining timber

View the Tectonics project ↗

The timber-milling footage documents a subtractive fabrication process: the robot carries a cutting tool along a programmed path to machine a timber member. It sits alongside the material and assembly trials in Tectonics of Stock Assemblies.

The physical workshop trials are distinct from the project’s full digital aggregation. The related project page explains the inventory, aggregation logic and structural evaluation.

03 / BioPol 3D Printing

Depositing material

View the BioPol workshop ↗

In the BioPol group workshop, the robot deposits a biopolymer composite along non-planar toolpaths. The videos connect the simulated motion to the physical behaviour of the freshly extruded material.

Trials with Brick 7 and Brick 1 show sagging and overhang conditions, while the speed comparison makes differences in deposition visible. These are fabrication experiments within a partial arch prototype, rather than a completed printed vault.

BioPol group: Luis Leon Escoda, Gabrielle Brooking, Naitik Sharma and Filip Munch Bak. CITA, Royal Danish Academy. Full project narrative and credits are on the BioPol page.
