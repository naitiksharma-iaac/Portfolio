This environmental study develops a Sydney building proposal through climate data, solar exposure, daylight, glare, wind and outdoor-comfort analysis.

Successive massing options respond to a humid subtropical climate by balancing summer shade, winter sun, natural ventilation and useful daylight.

Humid subtropical Sydney

Sydney is classified as humid subtropical (Cfa) in the source, with warm, relatively hot and humid summers, cooler winters and an acknowledged urban heat-island condition.

Direct, diffuse and global radiation

Direct solar radiation is highest during summer and lower during winter. The source compares direct, diffuse and global radiation with sky-cover conditions.

No local project media is currently present in the repository. The preserved source folder remains ready for original media.
