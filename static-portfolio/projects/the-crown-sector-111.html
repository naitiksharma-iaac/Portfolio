Facade optimisation

The Crown is a large residential development in Sector 111, Gurugram, designed by Studio Symbiosis. Its tower identity is organised through vertically growing, petal-inspired facade elements.

My documented contribution was facade optimisation. The case study focuses on translating repeated facade elements, changing curvature and local architectural conditions into a controlled parametric system.

A vertical petal language

The facade language reinterprets petals as vertically growing elements that give the towers a continuous identity. Their curvature shifts at lower levels to respond to arrival, shade and pedestrian conditions.

A repeated architectural element

A common facade element establishes continuity while its control geometry accommodates changes in height, orientation and adjoining conditions.
