Construction contributes 40% of global emissions and generates significant waste. Reusing salvaged timber offers an alternative, but reclaimed members cannot be treated as standardised stock.

Circular Hub investigates how reclaimed timber can become the input to a computational design system through material characterisation, connection rules, structural stress fields and graph representations.

Characterising irregular timber

The material passport extracts geometric features from salvaged members and organises irregular timber into families without erasing its differences.

Reversible connection rules

A construction kit defines multiple connection possibilities and translates the rule system into a reversible physical assembly logic.
