Facade optimisation

The Edition is a residential development in Sector 66, Gurugram, designed by Studio Symbiosis. Three twin high-rise towers use a branching, skeletal facade language that grows across balconies.

My documented contribution was facade optimisation: parameterising the repeated system, coordinating it with balcony geometry and retaining architectural continuity through local variation.

A branching skeletal system

The facade is conceived as a skeletal system that branches outward across the towers, producing a continuous vertical identity while responding to the residential floor plates.

From primary lines to local branches

Primary vertical elements establish the overall rhythm. Secondary branches negotiate balcony projections, perforations and the changing silhouette.
