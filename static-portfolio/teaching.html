Teaching

At Aerie Academy, Naitik teaches computational design through practical architectural exercises, helping students move from digital modelling fundamentals to confident parametric workflows.

Basics of Computational Design

This three-month Rhino and Grasshopper course introduces computational design from beginner to intermediate level. The teaching approach combines clear demonstrations, guided exercises and architectural applications so students can learn by making.

Course scope

  • Rhino interface and 3D modelling basics
  • Grasshopper fundamentals and parametric workflows
  • Form finding, complex geometry and organic facades
  • Architectural modelling, visualization and design exercises

Learning outcome

Students finish with a foundation for creating advanced architectural forms, understanding parametric relationships and developing computational design projects independently.
