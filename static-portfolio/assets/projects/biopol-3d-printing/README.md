# BioPol case study sources

The local workshop folder is `project-references/Biopol-3Dprinting_Workshop`.

- `Biopol Workshop.pptx`: 59 slides, group project presented 20 April 2026. Primary narrative and evidence.
- `Intro_Lecture.pptx`: 81 slides, Designing Behaviour: Adaptive Fabrication with Learning Systems, 16 February 2026. Background on material mixtures, learning framework and brief.

Narrative coverage: design challenge (workshop 3–8); global vault and arch studies (10–17); prototype and 12-part subdivision (18–23); slicing and interfaces (24–29); printed count (30); flow and velocity (34–39); sagging, overhang and infill (40–44); model configurations and simulations (45–52); speculative architectural visualisation (54).

The page distinguishes the complete proposed vault from the partial physical prototype: slide 30 records five printed bricks out of twelve. Material ingredients refer to the introductory framework, not a verified recipe for every specimen. Non-planar slicing benefits are design motivations, not measured strength claims. The team applies and evaluates the supplied learning framework; the page does not claim it built or trained the underlying infrastructure.

`source-manifest.json` maps exported images to the original images or slide media. Extracted deck images retain slide numbers in their intermediate filenames. All assets preserve proportions and are resized without enlargement. No source files were modified.
